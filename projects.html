2026 · Privacy & security platform

PrivacyTestLab

Live

Design · Full-stack build · Security

Most people have no idea what their browser silently reveals. PrivacyTestLab is a free testing platform that shows them — and explains what to do about it.

What I built
IP & DNS leak detection, WebRTC leak testing, browser fingerprint analysis, metadata remover, password generator, and network diagnostics (speed, ping & jitter).
How
PHP backend, vanilla JavaScript tooling on the client, hardened with .htaccess rules. Modular architecture, accessibility-first UI.
Why it's hard
A privacy site must practice what it preaches — no third-party trackers, no leaked user data, tools that run client-side wherever possible.
